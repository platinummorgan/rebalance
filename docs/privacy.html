Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 24, 2026

Privacy Summary

Wealth Dial ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. We do not collect your personal financial records.

Data Storage

  • Local Storage Only: Financial records stay on your device.
  • Encryption: Data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption on your device.
  • No Cloud Sync: We do not maintain cloud copies of your financial data.

Network Use (Limited)

  • Currency Rates: Optional exchange-rate lookups for currency conversion.
  • Purchase Verification: Pro purchase checks with app-store billing and our entitlement backend.
  • External Links: If you open links, your browser handles those requests.

What We Do Not Collect

  • No behavioral analytics or telemetry.
  • No ads or ad tracking.
  • No upload of accounts, liabilities, income, expenses, or portfolio composition to our servers.

Data Sharing

For purchase verification only, minimal metadata may be sent (anonymous app user ID, product ID, purchase token, package name). We do not sell personal data.

Third-Party Services

App-store billing and exchange-rate providers are governed by their own privacy policies.

Your Choices

You can clear local app data at any time from your device settings. For privacy questions, email admin@ripstuff.net.

GDPR & CCPA Compliance

We follow data-minimization principles, keep financial records local to your device, and limit network requests to optional service functionality.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

Contact Us
