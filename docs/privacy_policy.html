Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 24, 2026

Wealth Dial (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our privacy-first handling of your data.

Local-first data handling

  • Financial records stay on your device: accounts, liabilities, income, expenses, and settings are stored locally.
  • No behavioral analytics: we do not run analytics telemetry in the app.
  • No ads: no ad SDKs or ad tracking.

Limited network use

  • Currency conversion: optional exchange-rate requests for multi-currency features.
  • Purchase verification: app-store billing + entitlement verification for Pro access.
  • External links: opened only when you choose to open them.

Data sharing

For purchase verification, minimal metadata may be transmitted (anonymous app user ID, product ID, purchase token, package name). Your financial records are not uploaded to our servers. We do not sell personal data.

Third-party services

App-store billing services and exchange-rate providers are governed by their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can clear local app data at any time from your device settings. If you wish to contact us, email admin@ripstuff.net.

Contact

For privacy questions contact: admin@ripstuff.net
